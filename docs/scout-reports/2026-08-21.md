# Model Scout Report — 2026-08-21

Scope: locale-optimized ASR, two lenses. Requested after GH #49 surfaced
download size as a real user concern, and the maintainer's reframing: with the
external-models platform, MULTILINGUAL IS NO LONGER REQUIRED. Small
MONOLINGUAL models per locale are the interesting class.

- Lens A: Italian quality (maintainer is a native judge).
- Lens B: best quality within a SMALL class (working definition: 20-200MB int8
  download; this report uses "small" in that sense, NOT "smallest possible").

## Headline findings

1. Nothing new beats Distil-IT (4.3% WER) for Italian quality. The on-device
   Italian landscape is static. The new distil-large-v3.5 line is English-only;
   bofenghuang's multi7 experiment is WORSE than our current model on Italian
   by its own card's numbers (6.78% vs 6.15% mcv17).
2. SenseVoice-Small does NOT do Italian (zh/en/ja/ko/yue only). The initial
   scout claim of "50+ languages incl. Italian" was wrong; verified
   EMPIRICALLY: the sherpa-onnx export transcribes 32s of Italian as "."
   (empty output). That claim belongs to Fun-ASR-MLT, which stays on watch.
3. No small-class monolingual Italian model exists at all. Zero zipformer /
   CTC Italian exports in the sherpa ecosystem. The gap is real and is the
   single biggest opportunity (TASK-367).
4. The small-class recipe is proven: a 30M-param zipformer RNNT (32.6MB int8)
   won VLSP-2025 Vietnamese at 8-12% WER, public recipe
   (hynt/Zipformer-30M-RNNT-6000h). An Italian equivalent is the training
   target.
5. Other locales have importable small options today (quality UNVERIFIED):
   ES bookbot robust 27MB, DE whisper-tiny-de 59MB, DE/FR/EN kroko zipformer
   68MB (fp32, CC-BY-SA), RU vosk-based zipformer 70MB / 27MB. All map to
   already-supported import families (zero app changes).

## Process note

One scout agent's language claim (SenseVoice "50+ incl. Italian") was wrong
and would have produced a bad recommendation; it was caught by a 30-second
empirical harness run before anything shipped. Scout claims about language
coverage or WER must be verified empirically (eval harness or sherpa python)
before they reach users. A third scout agent (pre-reframing, multilingual
lens) never returned; its scope was superseded by the monolingual reframing.
